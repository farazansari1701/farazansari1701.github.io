Privacy & cookies

How information is used when you browse this personal portfolio, open my CV or certificates, or contact me about work and collaboration.

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Who is responsible?

This is the personal website of Faraz Ansari. Questions about personal information associated with it can be sent to contact@farazansari1701.com.

Information processed

  • Contact information: the name, email address and message you choose to send. Email links open your own email application. The site currently has no on-site enquiry database.
  • Technical information: GitHub Pages, the website host, and external resources may receive IP addresses, request details, browser and device information as needed to deliver the site.
  • Optional analytics: if permitted, Google Analytics 4 via Google Tag Manager measures visits and interactions such as CV opens, outbound link clicks and article engagement.
  • Browser preferences: the optional analytics decision is stored locally under fa-consent.

Purposes and lawful bases

Contact information is used to reply to your enquiry and discuss professional opportunities you raise. This generally relies on legitimate interests in responding to correspondence. Optional analytics uses consent. Essential site delivery and security may involve legitimate interests or other lawful bases relevant to the service provider.

Cookies and consent

Google Tag Manager loads on portfolio and blog pages. Analytics storage is set to denied by default. If you actively allow analytics, Google Analytics may set measurement cookies such as _ga and send website usage events. You can browse without allowing analytics. Some essential hosting requests and requests to external resources may still occur.

To change your selection, use Cookie settings in the portfolio footer or a blog footer. You can also clear this site's local storage and cookies in your browser. This policy page itself does not load the site's analytics scripts.

Providers and external links

GitHub Pages hosts the site; Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager provide optional measurement. Some blog pages load Google Fonts and CDN resources. If you click LinkedIn, Calendly, issuer verification or other external links, their own privacy terms apply. Your email service and any chosen booking provider also process information you decide to send.

These services may process information outside the UK under their own transfer arrangements. Further information: GitHub privacy, Google privacy, and Calendly privacy.

Retention

Messages should be kept only while needed to respond, maintain a continuing professional conversation, or meet any applicable obligations; records should be reviewed and deleted once no longer needed. Google Analytics retention follows its configured settings and your consent choices. You may ask for deletion by email.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may request access, correction or deletion, object to processing, or ask for restrictions. You can withdraw analytics consent at any time. Email contact@farazansari1701.com for privacy requests, or contact the UK Information Commissioner's Office if you wish to complain.

Scope: This notice describes the current website and identified integrations. It should be reviewed if new forms, advertising tools or other services are added, and against actual retention and provider settings.
