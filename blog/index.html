A place for the work, the world and everything in between.

Some lessons come from campaigns, reports and the challenge of making an idea work. Others come from a conversation on an ordinary day, a question that is hard to shake, or a moment that changes how I understand someone. I want to make space for both.

This blog brings together two parts of my life: what I learn as a digital marketer, and what I notice, question and care about as a person. Different subjects, but the same curiosity about people, choices and what makes a difference.

What work teaches me

Here, I write about digital marketing, SEO, AI, campaigns, CRM and automation through a practical lens. My HubSpot marketing automation reflection looks at how a customer journey fits together, while the CRM data quality guide explores the details that make those journeys dependable.

I also question the numbers behind decisions. My pieces on lead quality beyond cost per lead and marketing attribution focus on what we are really measuring, rather than the story a single metric seems to tell. For related reading, visit the marketing automation collection.

What life makes me think about

Outside work, the questions are often less tidy. Conversations, friendships, growing up between places and everyday encounters can lead me to reflect on belonging, dignity, fairness, education, empathy and freedom. Sometimes I arrive at an opinion; sometimes I leave with a better question.

My personal perspectives are a home for those first-person essays. They will grow from experiences I actually share and reflect on, not stories invented to fill a publishing schedule. I want to be thoughtful about other people's privacy and honest about what I know, what I feel and what I am still learning.

The thread connecting both

Whether I am exploring a marketing decision or a bigger question about society, I try to distinguish lived experience from interpretation and support factual claims with sources where appropriate. This is a personal space for curiosity, not a claim to have all the answers.
