About me

I'm Faraz.

The places that shaped me, and the questions I carry.

Explore my work ↗ Open my notebook ↗

The poems below say something about what draws me to poetry. Iqbal gives words to a longing for quiet; Frost to the promises that keep a person moving. I recognise something of myself in both.

My roots are in Varanasi, I grew up in Mussoorie, and I moved to the UK in 2019. Cambridge was where I studied; London is where I'm building my life now.

History, travel, art and music offer other ways into people's lives. They feed the questions I want to write about here: what makes us feel at home, how we treat one another, and what fairness and freedom mean in ordinary life.

2019: starting somewhere new

I moved to the UK in 2019. Cambridge became the place where I studied at Anglia Ruskin University; London later became the place where I began building my working life.

That move gave me a new kind of independence. It also made me think about the people, assumptions and values I carried with me, and the ones I began to reconsider. Staying connected to where I started and making my own choices have become part of the same experience.

The part work plays

I'm a Senior Digital Marketing Executive at OKI Europe, working across campaigns, SEO, automation and AI. Earlier, I worked with X-Forces Enterprise.

Work gives me another way to ask questions: what helps someone, what the evidence tells us and what a useful next step looks like. My portfolio covers the projects; my writing explores what I learn along the way.
