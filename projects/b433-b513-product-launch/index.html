The launch

OKI Europe announced the B433 and B513 on 30 September 2025, with availability across EMEA from October. The launch introduced two A4 monochrome, single-function printers for workplace printing, replacing the B432 and B512 models.

The product proposition centred on the practical demands of busy workplaces: dependable document printing, equipment that fits into limited space and straightforward maintenance. OKI’s announcement highlighted settings including retail, healthcare, logistics, legal and professional services.

My contribution focused on the communications supporting that introduction. I led the targeted email activity and automated follow-ups, bringing together the initial product message and continued communication around the launch.

The communication challenge

Making the product story relevant

Introducing two related printers means explaining their shared strengths and making the differences easy to understand. The public product information provides three useful ways to connect the specifications with everyday workplace needs.

01 / SPACE

Where the printer fits

Both models have a compact design and front access for maintenance. For a business working with a service counter, shared office or under-desk position, the space needed to use and maintain a printer is part of the buying decision.

02 / UPKEEP

What maintenance involves

Top-loading consumables and a user-replaceable fuser are practical features of the range. They give the product story a clear operational focus: how the equipment can be maintained during everyday use.

03 / CAPACITY

Which model suits the workload

The B513dn offers a higher print speed and greater paper capacity than the B433dn. Presenting those differences alongside the shared features helps a reader compare the models against the volume and pace of their printing.

The models at a glance

The full model names are B433dn and B513dn. The launch announcement and artwork use the shorter B433/B513 names. Both are A4 mono LED printers with automatic duplex printing; the main differences below are speed and paper capacity.
