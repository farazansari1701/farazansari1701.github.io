The campaign

MyOKI is OKI Europe’s online portal for customers managing their relationship with OKI products and support. The campaign work I coordinated was designed for a pan-European environment, where the same core proposition had to be delivered through multiple local market versions rather than one generic message.

The project combined audience segmentation, localisation and campaign operations in HubSpot. My responsibility was not simply to send one email: it was to help organise the campaign structure so reseller and end-customer communications could be handled separately while the wider programme stayed consistent.

The 31-record figure represents documented campaign scope, not a performance result. It is the number of localised HubSpot campaign records in the two core audience streams.

My contribution

  1. 01 / STRUCTURE

    Coordinating the localised campaign set-up

    I owned and coordinated the 31 localised HubSpot campaign records that made up the programme. At this scale, the operational challenge is keeping the local versions traceable while preserving a clear relationship to the parent campaign.

  2. 02 / SEGMENTATION

    Separating reseller and end-customer audiences

    I coordinated segmentation for two distinct audience groups rather than treating the entire database as one audience. This allowed the campaign operation to reflect the different roles resellers and end customers have in the MyOKI ecosystem.

  3. 03 / ASSETS

    Coordinating campaign assets across local versions

    I coordinated the campaign assets needed for delivery across the localised records. With multiple market versions, consistency becomes an operational requirement: the core proposition needs to remain recognisable while each local version is prepared for its intended audience.

  4. 04 / DELIVERY

    Keeping the campaign operation aligned

    I coordinated delivery across the campaign set so the individual local records remained part of one programme rather than becoming disconnected market activities. The work demonstrates the campaign-operations side of localisation as much as the creative side.

Why the audience split mattered

The two campaign streams were organised around different relationships with MyOKI. Keeping those audiences separate made the programme easier to manage and gave each stream a clearer role.

Reseller stream

Supporting the partner relationship

Resellers can play a role in managing customer devices when an end user opts in. That makes MyOKI relevant not only as a customer portal, but also within the wider service relationship between customers, devices and authorised partners.

End-customer stream

Helping customers manage ownership

For end customers, MyOKI provides a place to register and manage devices, access warranty information and support resources, and work with original OKI consumables. The campaign stream therefore speaks to the practical experience of owning and maintaining equipment.

Public product context

What MyOKI brings together

OKI’s current public materials describe MyOKI as a single online portal that brings together several parts of the ownership and support experience. These features provide useful context for why a localised campaign programme has value across different customer relationships.

01 / DEVICES

Registered device management

Users can keep track of registered OKI printers and manage multiple devices in one place.

02 / WARRANTY

Warranty information

The portal supports printer registration and access to key warranty information, including current warranty programmes for eligible products.

03 / SUPPORT

Support resources

Public MyOKI information includes access to manuals, drivers and support information alongside device details.

04 / CONSUMABLES

Original OKI consumables

Users can view registered original OKI consumables or add new items to their account, linking the portal with ongoing product ownership.

05 / REMINDERS

Ongoing communication

Current warranty information describes confirmation and reminder communications through MyOKI and email, reinforcing the portal’s role beyond initial registration.

06 / PARTNERS

Reseller involvement

Under current warranty terms, end users can opt in and select a reseller to manage their devices, which helps explain the relevance of a separate reseller audience stream.

Scope, evidence & limits

This project demonstrates campaign operations at European scale: structuring localised HubSpot records, separating audience streams and coordinating assets and delivery across multiple versions.

I have deliberately not published contact data, internal record IDs, unpublished email metrics, conversion figures, revenue attribution or other commercially sensitive information. The 31-record count and the 15/16 audience split describe campaign scope only.
