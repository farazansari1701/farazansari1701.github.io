The project

The Soldiering On Awards recognises achievements across the Armed Forces community, including the people and organisations supporting those who serve and have served. The awards form part of X-Forces (XF) CIC, the organisation behind X-Forces Enterprise.

My involvement began with logo redesign during my marketing internship in 2020 and developed into ongoing website content work and a new site launch in 2022. The work connected how the awards looked across their communications with how that identity appeared online.

The images in this case study show the previous identity at the 2019 finalists reception and the refreshed identity in the 2021 finalists announcement.

My role, across three stages

  1. 2020

    Logo & visual identity

    Marketing Intern · X-Forces Enterprise

    I contributed to redesigning the Soldiering On Awards logo. The wider refresh introduced an updated logo, colour and brand identity, carried across the website, social channels and event materials.

  2. 2021

    Keeping the website current

    Digital Marketing Assistant · X-Forces Enterprise

    I managed and supported day-to-day content updates on soldieringon.org using WordPress. This was part of my wider content work across the X-Forces Enterprise, Soldiering On Awards and X-Emergency Services websites.

  3. 2022

    Launching the new website

    Digital Marketing Executive · X-Forces Enterprise

    I worked with an external developer to launch a new Soldiering On Awards website, contributing from the marketing side to the website delivery.

The website work

The 2022 launch followed my ongoing content work on the existing awards site. It brought together the organisation’s marketing work and the external developer’s website delivery.

Across my wider Digital Marketing Executive role at X-Forces Enterprise, I also worked on CMS management, content changes, page creation, user experience and conversion improvements, and website performance tracking and reporting.

  • Brand application: the refreshed identity across the website, social channels and event materials.
  • Content management: day-to-day WordPress updates on the awards website during 2021.
  • Developer collaboration: working with an external developer on the new website launch in 2022.
