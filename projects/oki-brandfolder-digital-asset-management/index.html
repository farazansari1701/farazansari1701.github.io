The project

Brandfolder is a digital asset management (DAM) platform used to organise, control, distribute and measure digital assets. My portfolio records my involvement with OKI Europe’s official digital asset management environment from October 2023 onwards, with project management and SEO among the skills associated with the work.

The portfolio artwork shows a structured OKI Europe Brandfolder environment with asset areas for different audiences and uses, including distributors, resellers, SDRs and training. The value of that structure is not simply storage: it is making the right asset easier to find and distribute.

DAM context

Why a digital asset management platform matters

Brandfolder describes its platform as a way for marketers, creatives and other teams to organise, control, create, distribute and measure digital assets. This is a different problem from simply putting files in cloud storage.

01 / FIND

Search and discovery

Brandfolder supports asset search using metadata, taxonomy, tags and filters, helping users find the right file without knowing its exact folder path.

02 / SHARE

Controlled distribution

DAM tools support public or private sharing and permissions, which is useful when assets need to move between internal teams, partners and external users.

03 / GOVERN

One source of truth

Central asset management reduces the risk of teams working from outdated or duplicated files and makes brand assets easier to govern consistently.
