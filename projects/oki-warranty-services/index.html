The project

The warranty work sits at the intersection of acquisition, ownership and retention. A customer may first encounter warranty information while researching a printer, but the relationship continues after purchase through registration, MyOKI and ongoing use of genuine consumables.

My portfolio records responsibility for improving the visibility of OKI Warranty Services through SEO-led website updates, email marketing and digital campaign activity. I also created and maintained customer-facing content explaining the role of genuine OKI consumables in protecting device performance and print quality.

Current public warranty journey

From purchase to continued protection

OKI’s current UK warranty pages show how MyOKI connects the post-purchase journey: eligible printers are registered through the portal, and the Product Lifetime Warranty can be maintained by registering at least one original OKI toner each year, subject to programme terms and model eligibility.

01 / REGISTER

Register the printer

Eligible devices are registered through MyOKI using device details and proof of purchase.

02 / MAINTAIN

Register original toner

The current programme requires at least one original OKI toner to be registered each year for continued eligible warranty extension.

03 / REMIND

Keep the relationship active

OKI states that confirmations and reminders are provided through MyOKI and email, making warranty an ongoing lifecycle communication rather than a one-off page.

Current public programme details

What customers need to do

The current Product Lifetime Warranty proposition has specific eligibility and renewal rules. Making those rules understandable is central to effective warranty communication.

01 / 60 DAYS

Register within the window

OKI currently states that an eligible printer must be registered through MyOKI within 60 days of purchase. Registration requires the printer model, serial number, proof of purchase and customer contact details.

02 / YEARLY

Maintain eligibility annually

To keep the extended warranty active, at least one original OKI toner must be registered each year, including the year the device is first registered. Once verified, protection can be extended for another 12 months.

03 / MODEL LIMIT

Coverage depends on the printer

The programme applies to selected models, and the maximum warranty period varies by model. “Up to 7 years” is therefore a programme ceiling rather than a universal entitlement for every OKI printer.

OKI also publishes a standard 3-year warranty route as an alternative. Current conditions should always be checked on the official warranty pages because eligible models and programme rules can change.

Content strategy

The communication challenge is to make a technical support proposition understandable at the moment a customer needs it. That means answering practical questions: what needs to be registered, where the customer manages the warranty, what keeps an extended warranty active, and why original consumables matter.

The work therefore combines search intent, clear explanatory copy and lifecycle messaging rather than treating warranty as a purely legal or support-page topic.

From a content perspective, there are several different intents to cover: customers comparing warranty options before purchase, recent buyers trying to register a device, existing owners checking whether protection remains active, and customers looking for the role of original OKI toner in annual renewal. Treating those as separate information needs makes the content more useful than a single generic warranty page.

This also creates a natural relationship between SEO and email: search-led content helps customers find the right guidance when they need it, while lifecycle email can reinforce time-sensitive actions such as registration and annual renewal.
