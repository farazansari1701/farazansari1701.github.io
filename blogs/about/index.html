The work behind the writing

I’m a London-based digital marketer working across SEO, integrated campaigns, HubSpot, CRM and marketing automation at OKI Europe. My work brings together the content people see and the systems that support their journey, from finding a page to receiving a relevant follow-up.

I joined OKI Europe as a Digital Marketing Executive in October 2022 and progressed to Senior Digital Marketing Executive in October 2025. Before that, I worked with X-Forces Enterprise across digital marketing, website delivery and campaigns for the military community.

My journey began in Varanasi and took me to Cambridge to study at Anglia Ruskin University, before moving to London for work. Studying and working in the UK has shaped how I approach communication, collaboration and the practical decisions behind a campaign.

What you’ll find in my blogs

I write about the questions that come up when marketing strategy meets delivery: how to make an automated journey useful, what to check in a CRM, and how to judge campaign performance beyond a headline number.

Expect practical frameworks, reflections and clear explanations of the tools and decisions involved. I focus on steps you can apply to your own work, with sources where they help explain a method or put a lesson in context.

Where I share professional experience, I distinguish it from educational guidance. My aim is to make the thinking behind the work as useful as the outcome.

These articles reflect my own views and experience, rather than an official statement from OKI Europe.

How I approach these articles

I write to explain the decisions behind digital marketing work, rather than list software features. Each reflection identifies the situation, the responsibility, the action and the outcome I can support. That makes the context visible to readers who may work with different audiences, budgets or tools.

Articles distinguish my professional experience from general guidance. They identify the period a reflection relates to and link to relevant documentation or public professional records where useful. A career milestone provides context; it does not establish that one campaign caused that progression.

When verified performance figures are not available for a specific initiative, I describe the learning without inventing an uplift. My views are personal and do not represent an official statement from OKI Europe. You can start with the HubSpot marketing automation reflection or browse all published insights.
