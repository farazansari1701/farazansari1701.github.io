Digital marketing insights you can put to work

Good marketing connects the right message, a useful customer experience and a clear next step. Here, I share practical thinking on SEO, campaigns, HubSpot, CRM and marketing automation, informed by my work as a digital marketer.

These articles explore the decisions behind delivery: understanding an audience, connecting channels, improving information and deciding what success should mean. Each piece offers a focused starting point for your next review, campaign or conversation.

Build a more connected customer journey

Start with my HubSpot marketing automation reflection to explore how email, landing pages, CRM and follow-up work together. Use its practical checklist to review who a journey serves, what triggers it and what the recipient should do next.

Before building more automation, check the information behind it. The CRM data quality guide explains how to review identity, completeness, consistency and ownership so that the next action has a sound starting point.

Look beyond the headline metric

A lower acquisition cost is only part of the story. My article on B2B lead quality and cost per lead provides a framework for considering audience fit, qualification and the conversations that follow a campaign response.

The aim is to ask better questions of the numbers and make the next decision more useful. Practical examples and linked sources help explain the method without promising the same outcome for every business.

Understand the experience behind the advice

Experience-led articles follow the situation, task, action and result, with reflection on what I learnt. Educational guides explain a proposed approach you can adapt to your own work. I keep that distinction clear and use performance figures only where they can be supported.
